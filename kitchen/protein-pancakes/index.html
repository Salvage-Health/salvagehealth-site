Banana Protein Pancakes

Blender pancakes made from oats, banana, eggs and protein powder. No flour, no mix, still fluffy.

370calories26gprotein50gcarbs8gfat

Per serving. Estimates from USDA and label data, optional ingredients included.

Serves 215 minLevel 3
Open in the Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 1 cup (80 g) rolled oats
  • 1 ripe banana
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 scoop (30 g) protein powder
  • 1/2 cup (120 ml) milk
  • Berries for the top (optional)
  • 1 tsp baking powder, cinnamon, avocado oil spray

How to make it

  1. Prep everythingPeel the ripe banana and break it into chunks. Measure the 1 cup (80 g) of oats, 1 scoop (30 g) of protein powder, 1/2 cup (120 ml) of milk, 1 tsp of baking powder and a pinch of cinnamon. Rinse the berries and pat them dry.
  2. Blend the batterPut the oats, banana, 2 eggs, protein powder, milk, baking powder and cinnamon in a blender. Blend on high 30 to 60 seconds, until completely smooth with no oat bits. Let it sit 5 minutes so it thickens to the texture of regular pancake batter.
  3. Heat the panSet a large 12-inch (30 cm) nonstick skillet over medium-low heat for 2 minutes. Lightly coat it with avocado oil spray. A drop of water should sizzle gently, not jump and spit.
  4. Pour the pancakesPour 1/4 cup (60 ml) of batter per pancake, leaving room between them so they do not touch. Cook about 2 minutes, until bubbles pop on top and the edges look set and dry.
  5. Flip and finishSlide a thin spatula under each pancake and flip it in one quick motion. Cook 1 more minute, until the bottom is golden and the center springs back when pressed. Spray the pan again between batches.
  6. Stack and serveStack the pancakes on 2 plates and top with the berries. Eat them warm.
Chef's tipKeep the heat at medium-low. Protein powder and banana brown fast, so higher heat burns the outside before the middle cooks.
