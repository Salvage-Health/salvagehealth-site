Spaghetti Squash with Turkey Marinara

Roasted spaghetti squash strands under a hearty turkey meat sauce and parmesan. Big bowl, light on calories.

350calories27gprotein29gcarbs16gfat

Ingredients

  • 1 large spaghetti squash (about 3 lb / 1.4 kg)
  • 1 lb (454 g) lean ground turkey
  • 2 cups (500 g) marinara
  • 1/4 cup (25 g) grated parmesan (optional)
  • 2 cups (60 g) spinach (optional)
  • 1 tsp avocado oil; garlic, Italian seasoning, salt and pepper

How to make it

  1. Prep everythingHeat the oven to 400°F (200°C) and line a large rimmed sheet pan with parchment or foil. Mince 2 to 3 cloves of garlic. Measure the 2 cups (500 g) of marinara, 2 cups (60 g) of spinach and 1/4 cup (25 g) of parmesan.
  2. Halve the squashMicrowave the whole squash for 3 to 4 minutes to soften the skin so it is easier to cut. Set it on a damp towel so it will not roll, then cut it in half lengthwise with a large sharp knife. Scoop out the seeds and stringy bits with a spoon.
  3. Roast the squashRub the cut sides with the 1 tsp of avocado oil and sprinkle with salt. Place both halves cut side down on the sheet pan and roast 35 to 45 minutes, until a fork slides easily through the skin and the edges are lightly browned.
  4. Brown the turkeyAbout 20 minutes before the squash is done, put the 1 lb (454 g) of turkey in a large 12-inch (30 cm) skillet over medium-high heat. Break it into small crumbles and cook 7 to 9 minutes, until no pink remains and it reaches 160°F (71°C). Stir in the garlic, about 1 tsp of Italian seasoning, 1/2 tsp of salt and some pepper, and cook 1 minute until fragrant.
  5. Simmer the sauceAdd the marinara and spinach to the skillet and stir. Turn the heat to medium-low and simmer (small, lazy bubbles) for 10 minutes, stirring now and then, until the spinach is wilted and the sauce has thickened slightly.
  6. Make the strandsFlip the squash halves over using tongs or an oven mitt, since they are very hot. Drag a fork lengthwise through the flesh to pull it into long spaghetti-like strands, scraping all the way to the skin.
  7. Serve or storePile the squash strands into 4 bowls, top with the meat sauce, and sprinkle with the parmesan. Leftovers keep in sealed containers in the fridge for up to 4 days. Reheat in the microwave 2 minutes, and pour off any water that collects.
Chef's tipDo not overroast the squash. Pull it when a fork just slides through, because mushy squash turns watery instead of forming clean strands.
