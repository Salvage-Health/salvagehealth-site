One-Pan Chicken Burrito Skillet

Chicken, rice, beans, corn and salsa all cooked in one skillet, finished with melted cheese. Burrito bowl, fewer dishes.

540calories51gprotein61gcarbs9gfat

Serves 430 minLevel 2 Meal prep

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb (680 g) chicken breast, cubed
  • 1 cup (185 g) dry rice
  • 1 can (15 oz / 425 g) black beans, drained
  • 1 cup (150 g) corn (optional)
  • 1 cup (260 g) salsa
  • 1/2 cup (56 g) shredded cheese (optional)
  • 1 tsp avocado oil; chili powder, cumin, salt; 2 cups (480 ml) water

How to make it

  1. Prep everythingCut the 1 1/2 lb (680 g) chicken breast into 1-inch (2.5 cm) cubes and pat them dry with paper towels. Drain and rinse the can of black beans. Measure the 1 cup (185 g) dry rice, 1 cup (150 g) corn, 1 cup (260 g) salsa, 2 cups (480 ml) water and 1/2 cup (56 g) shredded cheese so everything is ready by the stove.
  2. Season the chickenPut the chicken in a bowl and sprinkle it with chili powder, cumin and a good pinch of salt. Toss with your hands or a spoon until every piece is lightly coated.
  3. Brown the chickenHeat the 1 tsp avocado oil in a large 12-inch (30 cm) skillet that has a lid over medium-high heat until the oil shimmers (looks wavy). Add the chicken in a single layer and cook about 5 minutes, stirring once or twice, until the outsides are white with some golden spots. It does not need to be cooked through yet, it finishes in the rice.
  4. Add rice and liquidsStir in the rice, salsa, black beans, corn and the 2 cups (480 ml) water. Scrape the bottom of the pan to loosen any browned bits. Turn the heat to high and bring it to a boil, where big bubbles break all over the surface, about 2 to 3 minutes.
  5. Cover and simmerPut the lid on and turn the heat down to low so it simmers (small, gentle bubbles). Cook 18 to 20 minutes without lifting the lid, until the rice is tender and the liquid is absorbed. Check that the chicken reaches 165°F (74°C) on an instant-read thermometer.
  6. Melt the cheeseTurn off the heat and sprinkle the cheese evenly over the top. Put the lid back on for 2 minutes, until the cheese is fully melted and gooey.
  7. Serve and storeScoop into bowls and serve warm, with extra salsa if you like. Leftovers keep in a sealed container in the fridge for up to 4 days. Reheat in the microwave with a splash of water, covered, for 1 to 2 minutes until hot.
Chef's tipDo not lift the lid while the rice simmers. Every peek lets steam escape, and that is how you end up with crunchy rice and a scorched bottom.
