High-Protein Breakfast Casserole

A whole week of breakfast in one 9 x 13 dish. Homemade turkey sausage, eggs, egg whites, cottage cheese, crispy potatoes, spinach and peppers under a layer of reduced-fat cheddar. Cut, box and reheat in a minute.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb (454 g) 93% lean ground turkey
  • 10 large eggs
  • 2 cups (480 ml) liquid egg whites (optional)
  • 1 cup (8 oz / 226 g) low-fat cottage cheese
  • 1 lb (454 g) potatoes, diced small (or frozen diced hash browns, thawed)
  • 2 packed cups (60 g) baby spinach, roughly chopped (optional)
  • 1 bell pepper, diced (optional)
  • 1 cup (4 oz / 113 g) reduced-fat shredded cheddar
  • Sausage seasoning: 1 tsp dried sage, 1 tsp salt, 1/2 tsp each black pepper, garlic powder and crushed fennel seed, 1/4 tsp each smoked paprika and red pepper flakes
  • Avocado oil spray; salt and pepper

How to make it

  1. Prep everythingHeat the oven to 375°F (190°C). Spray a 9 x 13-inch (23 x 33 cm) baking dish with avocado oil spray. Mix the sausage seasoning: 1 tsp dried sage, 1 tsp salt, 1/2 tsp each black pepper, garlic powder and crushed fennel seed, and 1/4 tsp each smoked paprika and red pepper flakes. Dice the 1 lb (454 g) potatoes and the bell pepper into 1/2-inch (1 cm) pieces and roughly chop the spinach.
  2. Brown the sausageHeat a large 12-inch (30 cm) skillet over medium-high heat with a light spray of avocado oil. Add the 1 lb (454 g) ground turkey and the seasoning, breaking it into small crumbles. Cook 7 to 8 minutes until browned with no pink left, 165°F (74°C). Scoop it into a bowl.
  3. Crisp the potatoesIn the same skillet over medium heat, add the potatoes with another spray of oil and a pinch of salt. Cook 8 to 10 minutes, stirring every couple of minutes, until golden on the outside and nearly tender. Add the bell pepper for the last 2 minutes and the spinach for the last 30 seconds, just until it wilts.
  4. Mix the eggsIn a large bowl, whisk the 10 eggs, 2 cups (480 ml) egg whites and 1 cup (226 g) cottage cheese with a pinch of pepper until the egg is fully broken up. The cottage cheese curds will stay a little lumpy, which is fine; they melt into creamy pockets as it bakes.
  5. Layer and bakeSpread the potatoes and vegetables in the baking dish, scatter the sausage over them, then pour the egg mix evenly over the top. Sprinkle the 1 cup (113 g) cheddar on top. Bake 40 to 45 minutes, until the center is set, a knife slid into the middle comes out clean, and the top is golden. The center should read 160°F (71°C).
  6. Rest, cut and storeLet it rest 10 minutes so it holds together, then cut into 8 squares. Store in containers for up to 4 days in the fridge, or wrap individually and freeze up to 2 months. Reheat a square in the microwave 60 to 90 seconds from the fridge, or 2 to 3 minutes from frozen.
Chef's tipAlways cook the potatoes before they go in. Raw potatoes won't finish in the time the eggs take to set, and you'll end up with crunchy potatoes or rubbery eggs.
