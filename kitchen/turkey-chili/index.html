Big Batch Turkey Chili

Lean turkey, two kinds of beans and tomatoes simmered with real spice. Freezes perfectly.

415calories40gprotein27gcarbs17gfat

Per serving. Estimates from USDA and label data, optional ingredients included.

Serves 645 minLevel 2 Meal prep
Open in the Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 2 lb (907 g) lean ground turkey
  • 1 can (15 oz / 425 g) kidney beans, drained
  • 1 can (15 oz / 425 g) black beans, drained
  • 2 cans (14.5 oz / 411 g each) diced tomatoes
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 bell pepper, diced (optional)
  • Shredded cheese to top (optional)
  • Greek yogurt to top (optional)
  • 1 tbsp avocado oil; 3 tbsp chili powder, 2 tsp cumin, 1 tsp oregano, garlic, salt

How to make it

  1. Prep everythingDice the onion and bell pepper into 1/2-inch (1 cm) pieces and mince the garlic. Drain and rinse both cans of beans. Mix the 3 tbsp chili powder, 2 tsp cumin, 1 tsp oregano and a pinch of salt in a small bowl, and open the 2 cans of diced tomatoes.
  2. Soften the vegetablesHeat the 1 tbsp avocado oil in a large 6-quart (5.7 L) pot or Dutch oven over medium heat. Add the onion and pepper and cook 5 minutes, stirring now and then, until soft and the onion looks see-through.
  3. Brown the turkeyTurn the heat to medium-high and add the 2 lb (907 g) ground turkey. Cook 7 to 8 minutes, breaking it into small crumbles, until no pink is left and it reaches 165°F (74°C).
  4. Toast the spicesStir in the garlic and the spice mix. Cook 1 minute, stirring constantly, until it smells rich and fragrant. Toasting the spices this way wakes up their flavor.
  5. Add tomatoes and beansPour in the diced tomatoes with their juice, both cans of beans and 1 cup (240 ml) water. Stir, scraping up anything stuck to the bottom, and turn the heat to high to bring it to a boil.
  6. Simmer the chiliLower the heat to medium-low so it simmers (steady gentle bubbles) and cook uncovered 25 to 30 minutes, stirring every 5 minutes or so. It is ready when it has thickened and a spoon dragged through leaves a trail. Taste and add more salt if needed.
  7. Serve and storeLadle into bowls and top with shredded cheese and a dollop of Greek yogurt. Cool leftovers, then refrigerate up to 4 days or freeze up to 3 months. Reheat on the stove over medium heat or in the microwave until steaming, adding a splash of water if it is too thick.
Chef's tipSalt at the end, not the start. Chili cooks down and concentrates, so tasting after simmering keeps it from ending up too salty.
