Cabbage-Wrapped Shredded Chicken Enchiladas

All the enchilada flavor with cabbage leaves standing in for tortillas. Saucy shredded chicken, melted Monterey Jack, a charred cheesy top, and over 60 grams of protein for under 500 calories.

Ingredients

  • 2 3/4 lb (1.25 kg) cooked, shredded chicken breast, from about 3 1/2 lb (1.6 kg) raw
  • 1 large head green cabbage, for 16 large leaves
  • 2 cans (10 oz / 283 g each) Old El Paso Original Enchilada Sauce
  • 12 oz (340 g) Monterey Jack, shredded, about 3 cups
  • 1 tsp each cumin, chili powder, garlic powder and onion powder; salt and pepper; avocado oil spray

How to make it

  1. Prep everythingHeat the oven to 375°F (190°C) and bring a big pot of water to a boil. Shred the 2 3/4 lb (1.25 kg) of cooked chicken with two forks if it is not already shredded, and shred the 12 oz (340 g) of Monterey Jack. Measure 1 tsp each of cumin, chili powder, garlic powder and onion powder, and open the 2 cans of enchilada sauce.
  2. Soften the cabbageCut the core out of the cabbage with a small knife. Lower the whole head into the boiling water and cook 3 to 5 minutes, peeling off the outer leaves with tongs as they turn soft and bendy, and keep going until you have 16. Pat them dry and shave down the thick center rib of each leaf with a knife so it rolls easily.
  3. Mix the fillingIn a large bowl, mix the chicken with the spices, a pinch of salt and pepper, 1 cup (240 ml) of the enchilada sauce and 1 cup (about 4 oz / 113 g) of the cheese. Stir until every bit of chicken is coated.
  4. Prepare the dishSpray a 9 x 13-inch (23 x 33 cm) baking dish with avocado oil. Spread a thin layer of enchilada sauce over the bottom, just enough to cover it.
  5. Roll the enchiladasSpoon a line of filling across each cabbage leaf, fold in the sides and roll it up tightly. Lay the rolls seam side down in the dish in two rows of 8.
  6. Bake coveredPour the rest of the sauce evenly over the rolls. Cover tightly with foil and bake 25 minutes, until the sauce is bubbling at the edges.
  7. Bake with cheeseRemove the foil and scatter the remaining cheese over the top. Bake another 15 to 20 minutes, until the cheese is bubbling and browned in spots, then rest 5 minutes so the sauce thickens.
  8. Serve and storeServe 2 rolls per person. Store leftovers in sealed containers in the fridge up to 4 days. Reheat in the microwave for 2 to 3 minutes, or covered in a 350°F (175°C) oven for about 15 minutes, until hot in the center.
Chef's tipShave down the thick rib on every cabbage leaf. Skipping it is the number one reason leaves crack and rolls fall apart.
