Turkey Stuffed Peppers

Bell peppers packed with seasoned turkey, rice and tomatoes, baked under melted cheese.

370calories29gprotein37gcarbs13gfat

Per serving. Estimates from USDA and label data, optional ingredients included.

Serves 450 minLevel 3 Meal prep
Open in the Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 4 large bell peppers, tops cut off and seeded
  • 1 lb (454 g) lean ground turkey
  • 1/2 cup (93 g) dry rice, cooked
  • 1 can (14.5 oz / 411 g) diced tomatoes
  • 1/2 onion, diced (optional)
  • 1/2 cup (56 g) shredded cheese (optional)
  • Garlic, Italian seasoning or cumin, salt and pepper

How to make it

  1. Prep everythingHeat the oven to 375°F (190°C). Cook the 1/2 cup (93 g) of dry rice ahead with 1 cup (240 ml) of water: bring to a boil, cover, and simmer on low 15 to 18 minutes. Cut the tops off the 4 peppers, pull out the seeds and white ribs, and dice the 1/2 onion into 1/4-inch (6 mm) pieces. Mince 2 cloves of garlic and open the can of tomatoes.
  2. Stand the peppersStand the peppers upright in a baking dish that holds them snugly, like an 8x8-inch (20x20 cm) or 9x13-inch (23x33 cm) dish. If one tips over, trim a thin slice off its bottom so it sits flat, without cutting a hole.
  3. Brown the turkeyPut the 1 lb (454 g) of turkey and the onion in a large 12-inch (30 cm) skillet over medium-high heat. Break the meat into small crumbles with a wooden spoon and cook 7 to 9 minutes, until no pink remains, the onion is soft, and the meat reaches 165°F (74°C). Stir in the garlic, about 1 tsp of Italian seasoning or cumin, 1/2 tsp of salt and some pepper, and cook 1 more minute until fragrant.
  4. Make the fillingTurn off the heat. Stir in the cooked rice and half the can of diced tomatoes until everything is evenly mixed. Taste and add a pinch more salt if it tastes flat.
  5. Fill and bake coveredSpoon the filling into the peppers, packing it in gently and mounding it on top. Spoon the rest of the tomatoes over the tops. Cover the dish tightly with foil and bake 30 minutes, until the peppers are softened.
  6. Add cheese and finishCarefully remove the foil (watch for steam) and sprinkle the 1/2 cup (56 g) of cheese over the peppers. Bake uncovered 10 more minutes, until the cheese is melted and bubbly and a knife slides easily into the pepper wall.
  7. Serve or storeLet them rest 5 minutes, then serve 1 pepper per person. Leftovers keep in a sealed container in the fridge for up to 4 days. Reheat in the microwave 2 to 3 minutes, until hot in the center.
Chef's tipKeep the foil on for the first 30 minutes. That trapped steam is what softens the peppers, and skipping it leaves them crunchy and raw-tasting.
