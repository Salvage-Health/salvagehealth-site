Creamy Tuscan Chicken

Golden seared chicken simmered in a rich parmesan cream sauce with sun-dried tomatoes and spinach, no pasta needed. Each serving has about 44 g protein and 7 g carbs and reheats like a dream.

425calories45gprotein7gcarbs24gfat

Serves 430 minLevel 2 Meal prep

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb (680 g) boneless skinless chicken breast (2 large breasts)
  • 1/2 cup (120 ml) heavy cream
  • 1/2 cup (120 ml) chicken broth
  • 1/2 cup (1.75 oz / 50 g) grated parmesan
  • 1/3 cup (1.4 oz / 40 g) sun-dried tomatoes in oil, drained and chopped
  • 4 packed cups (4 oz / 120 g) fresh baby spinach
  • 1 tbsp (15 ml) avocado oil
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp salt, 1/2 tsp pepper, 1 tsp oregano, 1/2 tsp paprika, pinch of chili flakes (optional)

How to make it

  1. Prep everythingSlice each chicken breast in half horizontally to make 4 thin cutlets about 1/2 inch (1 cm) thick, so they cook evenly. Pat dry and season both sides with the salt, pepper, oregano and paprika. Mince the garlic, chop the sun-dried tomatoes and grate the parmesan.
  2. Sear the chickenHeat the avocado oil in a large 12-inch (30 cm) skillet over medium-high heat until it shimmers. Add the cutlets and cook 4 to 5 minutes per side without moving them, until deep golden and 165°F (74°C) inside. Move them to a plate.
  3. Build the sauceLower the heat to medium, add the garlic and sun-dried tomatoes and stir for 1 minute until fragrant. Pour in the broth and scrape up the brown bits on the bottom of the pan with a wooden spoon (that is deglazing, and it is where the flavor lives). Stir in the cream and simmer 2 to 3 minutes until it lightly coats the spoon.
  4. Add the cheese and spinachTurn the heat to low and stir in the parmesan a little at a time until smooth. Add the spinach by handfuls and stir for 1 to 2 minutes until just wilted.
  5. Finish and serveReturn the chicken and any juices to the pan and spoon the sauce over it for 1 minute to rewarm. Serve with extra parmesan, or over cauliflower rice or zucchini noodles. Portion into 4 containers; it keeps 4 days in the fridge or 2 months frozen, and reheats in the microwave at 50% power for 2 to 3 minutes, stirring halfway.
Chef's tipAdd the parmesan over low heat and off the boil. High heat makes the cheese clump and the cream sauce turn grainy and oily.
