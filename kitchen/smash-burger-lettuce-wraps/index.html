Bunless Smash Burger Lettuce Wraps

Crispy-edged smashed patties with melty cheese, pickles and a tangy Greek yogurt burger sauce, all wrapped in cold, crunchy lettuce. You get about 44 g protein and only 8 g carbs per serving, and you won't miss the bun.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb (680 g) 93% lean ground beef
  • 4 slices American or cheddar cheese (3 oz / 84 g)
  • 1 head iceberg or butter lettuce (about 10 oz / 300 g), leaves separated
  • 1/2 cup (60 g) dill pickle chips, plus 1 tbsp pickle juice for the sauce
  • 1/2 small onion (2 oz / 60 g), sliced paper thin
  • 1/2 cup (120 g) plain nonfat Greek yogurt
  • 1 tomato, sliced (optional)
  • 1 tbsp (15 g) yellow mustard (or 1 tbsp sugar-free ketchup; regular ketchup adds sugar)
  • Avocado oil spray for the skillet
  • 1 tsp salt, 1/2 tsp pepper, 1/2 tsp paprika, 1/2 tsp garlic powder

How to make it

  1. Prep everythingSeparate the lettuce into large cup-shaped leaves, rinse and pat dry. Slice the onion paper thin and the tomato into rounds. Divide the beef into 8 loose 3-oz (85 g) balls without packing them tight, and keep them in the fridge until the pan is hot.
  2. Mix the burger sauceIn a small bowl stir together the Greek yogurt, mustard, 1 tbsp pickle juice, the paprika and garlic powder, and a pinch of salt. Taste and add more mustard or pickle juice until it is tangy enough for you.
  3. Heat the panSet a large cast iron or heavy 12-inch (30 cm) skillet over high heat for 3 to 4 minutes, until a drop of water sizzles away instantly. Mist it lightly with avocado oil spray.
  4. Smash the pattiesPlace 3 or 4 beef balls in the pan, lay a square of parchment on top and press each one flat with a sturdy spatula to about 1/4 inch (6 mm) thick. That is the smash: it gives you lacy, crispy brown edges. Sprinkle with salt and pepper and cook 2 minutes without moving them until the edges are deep brown.
  5. Flip and melt the cheeseScrape under each patty with the spatula, flip, and lay a half slice of cheese on top. Cook 1 minute more until the cheese melts and the beef reaches 160°F (71°C). Repeat with the remaining beef.
  6. Wrap and serveStack two patties in a double layer of lettuce leaves, then add pickles, onion, tomato and a big spoonful of burger sauce. Wrap it up like a taco and eat right away; the patties are best fresh, but cooked patties keep 3 days in the fridge.
Chef's tipKeep the beef cold and press it flat in the first 30 seconds only. Pressing later squeezes out the juices and gives you dry, gray patties instead of crispy ones.
