Chili-Lime Chicken Rice Bowls

Charred chicken thighs, black beans, corn and peppers over rice with a hit of lime. Four lunches done in one go.

670calories55gprotein79gcarbs14gfat

Per serving. Estimates from USDA and label data, optional ingredients included.

Serves 435 minLevel 3 Meal prep

Ingredients

  • 2 lb (907 g) boneless, skinless chicken thighs
  • 1 1/2 cups (280 g) dry jasmine rice
  • 1 can (15 oz / 425 g) black beans, drained
  • 1 cup (150 g) corn
  • 1 bell pepper, diced
  • 2 limes
  • Handful of cilantro, chopped (optional)
  • 1 tbsp avocado oil
  • 2 tsp chili powder, 1 tsp cumin, salt and pepper

How to make it

  1. Prep everythingRinse the 1 1/2 cups (280 g) jasmine rice in a fine strainer under cold water until the water runs mostly clear. Pat the 2 lb (907 g) chicken thighs dry with paper towels. Dice the bell pepper into 1/2-inch (1 cm) pieces, drain and rinse the black beans, chop the cilantro, and cut both limes in half. If your corn is frozen, let it thaw on the counter while you work.
  2. Cook the ricePut the rice in a medium saucepan with 2 1/4 cups (530 ml) water and a pinch of salt. Bring it to a boil on high, then cover, turn the heat to low and cook 15 minutes. Turn off the heat and leave the lid on for 10 more minutes, then fluff with a fork.
  3. Season the chickenIn a large bowl, toss the chicken with the 2 tsp chili powder, 1 tsp cumin, about 1 tsp salt, 1/2 tsp pepper, half the avocado oil (1 1/2 tsp) and the juice of 1 lime. Make sure every piece is coated.
  4. Sear the chickenHeat a large 12-inch (30 cm) cast-iron or nonstick skillet over medium-high heat for 2 minutes. Lay the thighs flat with space between them and sear (cook hard and fast without moving it) for 5 to 6 minutes per side, until deeply browned and they release easily from the pan. They are done at 175°F (79°C) in the thickest part. Cook in two batches if they do not fit in one layer, or use a grill on medium-high for the same time.
  5. Rest and sliceMove the chicken to a cutting board and let it rest 5 minutes so the juices settle back in. Slice it into 1/2-inch (1 cm) strips.
  6. Char the veggiesIn the same skillet over medium-high heat, add the rest of the avocado oil (1 1/2 tsp), the diced pepper and the 1 cup (150 g) corn. Cook 4 to 5 minutes, stirring every minute or so, until the pepper softens and both have brown, toasty spots. Stir in the beans and a pinch of salt and cook 1 to 2 minutes until hot.
  7. Build the bowlsDivide the rice between 4 bowls or meal prep containers, then add the beans and veg and top with the chicken. Squeeze the second lime over everything and finish with cilantro. Stored in the fridge, these keep 4 days; reheat covered in the microwave for 2 to 2 1/2 minutes with a splash of water.
Chef's tipOnce the chicken hits the pan, leave it alone. Moving it early tears the crust and you lose the char that makes this bowl taste like it came off a grill.
