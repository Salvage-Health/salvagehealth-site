Korean-Style Beef Bowls

Sweet and savory ground beef with garlic, ginger and soy over rice. Faster than takeout and a lot leaner.

580calories43gprotein71gcarbs13gfat

Per serving. Estimates from USDA and label data, optional ingredients included.

Serves 420 minLevel 2 Meal prep
Open in the Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb (680 g) 93% lean ground beef
  • 1 1/2 cups (280 g) dry rice
  • 1/4 cup (60 ml) low-sodium soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp honey or brown sugar
  • 1 cucumber, sliced (optional)
  • 2 cups (180 g) steamed broccoli (optional)
  • 4 cloves garlic, 1 tsp ginger (fresh or ground), red pepper flakes

How to make it

  1. Prep everythingStart the 1 1/2 cups (280 g) dry rice now using the package directions, since it takes the longest. Mince the 4 garlic cloves, and grate the ginger if fresh. Slice the cucumber into thin rounds, about 1/8 inch (3 mm), and steam the broccoli until bright green if it is not ready yet.
  2. Mix the sauceIn a small bowl, whisk the 1/4 cup (60 ml) low-sodium soy sauce, 2 tbsp honey or brown sugar, garlic, 1 tsp ginger and a pinch of red pepper flakes until the honey dissolves.
  3. Brown the beefHeat a large 12-inch (30 cm) nonstick skillet over high heat. Add the 1 1/2 lb (680 g) ground beef and cook 6 to 7 minutes, breaking it into small crumbles, until no pink remains, the edges are browned and it reaches 160°F (71°C).
  4. Drain the fatTilt the pan and spoon out any pooled fat into a heatproof bowl. Never pour hot fat down the sink.
  5. Glaze with sauceLower the heat to medium and pour in the sauce. Simmer (gentle bubbling) 2 minutes, stirring, until the sauce cooks down and the beef looks shiny and glossy.
  6. Build the bowlsDivide the rice among 4 bowls, then top with the beef, cucumber and broccoli. Store leftovers in sealed containers in the fridge up to 4 days, keeping the cucumber separate. Reheat the beef, rice and broccoli in the microwave 1 to 2 minutes until hot.
Chef's tipGet the pan truly hot and leave the beef alone for a minute before breaking it up. That brown crust is where all the flavor comes from.
