Greek Chicken Bowls

Lemon-oregano chicken over rice with cucumber, tomato, red onion, feta and a Greek yogurt tzatziki.

655calories65gprotein65gcarbs13gfat

Per serving. Estimates from USDA and label data, optional ingredients included.

Serves 435 minLevel 3 Meal prep
Open in the Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 2 lb (907 g) chicken breast
  • 1 1/2 cups (280 g) dry rice
  • 1 large cucumber
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1/2 red onion, thinly sliced (optional)
  • 1 cup (227 g) nonfat Greek yogurt
  • 1 lemon
  • 1/3 cup (50 g) crumbled feta (optional)
  • 1 tbsp avocado oil; garlic, oregano, salt and pepper

How to make it

  1. Prep everythingRinse the 1 1/2 cups (280 g) rice in a strainer until the water runs mostly clear. Grate a quarter of the cucumber on the large holes of a box grater and dice the rest into 1/2-inch (1 cm) pieces. Chop the 2 tomatoes into 1/2-inch (1 cm) pieces, thinly slice the 1/2 red onion, and juice the lemon into a small bowl. Mince 2 to 3 garlic cloves (or use garlic powder) and pat the 2 lb (907 g) chicken breast dry.
  2. Cook the ricePut the rice in a medium saucepan with 2 1/4 cups (530 ml) water and a pinch of salt. Bring it to a boil on high, then cover, turn the heat to low and cook 15 minutes. Turn off the heat and leave it covered 10 more minutes, then fluff with a fork.
  3. Season the chickenIn a bowl, toss the chicken with the 1 tbsp avocado oil, half the lemon juice, most of the garlic (save a little for the tzatziki), about 1 tsp oregano, 1 tsp salt and 1/2 tsp pepper. Make sure every piece is coated.
  4. Sear the chickenHeat a large 12-inch (30 cm) skillet or grill pan over medium-high heat for 2 minutes. Lay the chicken in and sear (cook hard and fast without moving it) 6 to 7 minutes per side, until golden brown and it releases easily from the pan. It is done when the thickest part reads 165°F (74°C). You can also grill it on medium-high for the same time.
  5. Rest and sliceMove the chicken to a cutting board and rest it 5 minutes, then slice it into 1/2-inch (1 cm) strips.
  6. Make the tzatzikiSqueeze the grated cucumber hard in your hands or a clean towel over the sink to get the water out. Stir it into the 1 cup (227 g) Greek yogurt with the saved garlic, a pinch of salt and the rest of the lemon juice. Tzatziki is a cool, tangy Greek yogurt and cucumber sauce.
  7. Build the bowlsDivide the rice between 4 bowls and top with the chicken, diced cucumber, tomato and red onion. Spoon on the tzatziki and sprinkle with the 1/3 cup (50 g) feta. For meal prep, keep the tzatziki in a separate small container; bowls keep 4 days in the fridge, and only the chicken and rice should be reheated.
Chef's tipSqueeze the grated cucumber until it is nearly dry. Skip this and your tzatziki turns thin and watery within an hour.
