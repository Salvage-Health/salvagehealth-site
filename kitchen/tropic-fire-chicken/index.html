Tropic Fire Grilled Chicken

A full 24-hour marinade of pineapple, fresh jalapeño, cilantro, garlic, ginger, onion, ancho chile and lime, then grilled hot for a deep caramelized crust and heavy grill marks. Sweet heat, bright citrus and a little smoke in every bite.

Ingredients

  • 2 lb (907 g) boneless, skinless chicken thighs
  • 1 cup (240 ml) pineapple juice (macros count the 1/4 cup or so that clings to the chicken)
  • 2 limes, juiced, plus more to serve
  • 1 to 2 fresh jalapeños, stems off (keep the seeds for more heat) (optional)
  • 1 cup (about 1 oz / 30 g) cilantro, stems and all, plus more to garnish (optional)
  • 4 green onions (optional)
  • 1/2 white onion, roughly chopped (optional)
  • 1-inch (2.5 cm) piece fresh ginger, peeled (or 1 tsp ground) (optional)
  • 6 cloves fresh garlic
  • 2 tbsp ancho chile powder (or 2 dried ancho chiles, stemmed, seeded and soaked)
  • 1 tbsp avocado oil; 2 tsp salt; 1 tsp black pepper

How to make it

  1. Prep everythingJuice the 2 limes and cut a couple of extra limes into halves for serving. Peel the 1-inch (2.5 cm) piece of ginger, peel the 6 garlic cloves, roughly chop the 1/2 white onion, cut the 4 green onions into a few pieces, and pull the stems off the 1 to 2 jalapeños. Measure the 1 cup (240 ml) of pineapple juice, 1 cup (30 g) of cilantro, 2 tbsp of ancho chile powder, 1 tbsp of avocado oil, 2 tsp of salt and 1 tsp of black pepper.
  2. Blend the marinadePut everything from the prep step except the chicken into a blender. Blend on high for about 1 minute, until smooth with no big chunks.
  3. Marinate 24 hoursPlace the 2 lb (907 g) of chicken thighs in a large zip-top bag or container and pour the marinade over them. Massage it into the meat so every piece is coated, then refrigerate a full 24 hours, turning it once or twice. Do not go much past 24 hours, because the pineapple breaks down the meat and the texture turns mushy.
  4. Dry the surfaceTake the chicken out of the fridge 20 minutes before grilling. Let the extra marinade drip off and lightly pat the tops with a paper towel, since a drier surface is what builds the crust. Throw away the used marinade, because it has touched raw chicken.
  5. Heat the grillHeat the grill to very hot, 450 to 500°F (230 to 260°C), with the lid closed for about 10 to 15 minutes. Clean the grates, then oil them by rubbing with a paper towel dipped in avocado oil, held in long tongs.
  6. Grill the thighsLay the thighs on the grates and leave them alone for 5 to 6 minutes, until they release easily and have deep caramelized grill marks. Flip and cook another 5 to 6 minutes, until a thermometer reads 175°F (79°C) in the thickest part. If the outside darkens before the inside is done, move them to a cooler part of the grill to finish.
  7. Rest and serveRest the chicken 5 minutes on a plate so the juices settle. Pile it on a platter, scatter chopped cilantro over the top and serve with lime halves for squeezing. Leftovers keep 4 days in a sealed container in the fridge, and reheat well in a skillet over medium heat or the microwave.
Chef's tipDo not flip the thighs early. If they stick, they are not ready yet, and waiting until they release on their own is what gives you those deep grill marks.
