Buffalo Chicken Dip Stuffed Celery and Peppers

Creamy, tangy buffalo chicken dip made lighter with Greek yogurt, then piled into crunchy celery and mini sweet peppers. Big game-day flavor with about 50 g protein and 8 g carbs per serving.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups (1 lb / 454 g) cooked shredded chicken breast (rotisserie works)
  • 3/4 cup (170 g) plain nonfat Greek yogurt
  • 2 oz (56 g) cream cheese, softened
  • 1/2 cup (120 ml) buffalo hot sauce (like Frank's RedHot)
  • 1 cup (4 oz / 113 g) shredded cheddar or mozzarella
  • 6 celery stalks (8 oz / 240 g), cut into 3-inch (7.5 cm) pieces
  • 8 mini sweet peppers (7 oz / 200 g), halved and seeded
  • 2 green onions, sliced (optional)
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder, 1/2 tsp paprika, pinch of salt

How to make it

  1. Prep everythingPreheat the oven to 375°F (190°C). Shred the chicken with two forks into bite-size strands. Cut the celery into 3-inch (7.5 cm) pieces, halve and seed the mini peppers, slice the green onions, and set the cream cheese out to soften for 10 minutes.
  2. Mix the dipIn a large bowl, stir the softened cream cheese until smooth, then mix in the Greek yogurt, hot sauce, garlic powder and paprika until no lumps remain. Fold in the chicken and half the shredded cheese.
  3. Bake the dipSpread the dip in a small 8 x 8-inch (20 x 20 cm) baking dish and top with the rest of the cheese. Bake 15 to 20 minutes, until bubbling at the edges and hot in the center, 165°F (74°C).
  4. Stuff the veggiesLet the dip cool 5 minutes so it firms up a little. Spoon it generously into the celery pieces and pepper halves, and top with green onions.
  5. Serve and storeServe warm or cold, about a quarter of the dip with a mix of celery and peppers per person. For meal prep, store the dip and the cut veggies in separate containers and fill right before eating; the dip keeps 4 days in the fridge and reheats in 60 to 90 seconds in the microwave. Freezing is not recommended since the yogurt turns grainy.
Chef's tipBeat the cream cheese smooth before adding the yogurt and hot sauce. Cold cream cheese stays in little lumps that never melt out, even in the oven.
