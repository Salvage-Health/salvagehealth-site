Philly Cheesesteak Stuffed Peppers

Thin-sliced sirloin and sweet onions piled into tender bell pepper boats and smothered in melted provolone. Every serving packs about 50 g protein with around 10 g carbs, and they reheat perfectly.

490calories48gprotein10gcarbs29gfat

Serves 440 minLevel 3 Meal prep

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb (680 g) sirloin steak, sliced very thin
  • 4 medium green or red bell peppers (1 lb / 450 g), halved and seeded
  • 1/2 medium onion (2.5 oz / 75 g), thinly sliced
  • 8 slices provolone (5.6 oz / 160 g)
  • 1 tbsp (15 ml) low-sodium soy sauce (optional)
  • 1 tbsp (15 ml) avocado oil, plus spray for the peppers
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp salt, 1/2 tsp pepper, 1/2 tsp garlic powder

How to make it

  1. Prep everythingPreheat the oven to 400°F (200°C). Put the steak in the freezer for 15 minutes, then slice it as thin as you can against the grain (across the lines of muscle), and cut the slices into 2-inch (5 cm) pieces. Halve the peppers lengthwise, remove seeds and ribs, and thinly slice the onion and mince the garlic.
  2. Par-bake the peppersSet the pepper halves cut side up in a 9 x 13-inch (23 x 33 cm) baking dish, mist with avocado oil spray and sprinkle with a pinch of salt. Bake 10 minutes, until they just start to soften.
  3. Cook the onionsWhile the peppers bake, heat 1 tsp avocado oil in a large 12-inch (30 cm) skillet over medium heat. Add the onion and cook 6 to 7 minutes, stirring now and then, until soft and golden. Move it to a bowl.
  4. Sear the steakTurn the heat to high, add the remaining oil and the steak in a single layer, and season with the salt, pepper and garlic powder. Cook 2 to 3 minutes, stirring once, until browned with no pink, at least 145°F (63°C), then stir in the garlic, soy sauce and onion mixture for 30 seconds.
  5. Stuff and bakeLay half a provolone slice inside each pepper, pack in the steak filling, and top each with another half slice. Bake 12 to 15 minutes, until the peppers are tender and the cheese is bubbly and browned in spots.
  6. Serve and storeServe 2 halves per person. Pack into containers; they keep 4 days in the fridge or 2 months frozen (thaw overnight first). Reheat covered in the microwave for 2 to 3 minutes, or at 350°F (175°C) in the oven for 15 minutes.
Chef's tipFreeze the steak for 15 minutes before slicing. Partly frozen beef cuts into paper-thin strips that cook fast and stay tender, instead of thick chewy chunks.
