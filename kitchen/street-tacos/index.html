Chicken Street Tacos

Small corn tortillas, juicy chopped chicken, onion, cilantro and lime. Taco night that still fits your macros.

410calories39gprotein40gcarbs10gfat

Per serving. Estimates from USDA and label data, optional ingredients included.

Serves 425 minLevel 3
Open in the Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb (680 g) boneless, skinless chicken thighs
  • 12 small corn tortillas
  • 1/2 white onion, finely diced
  • Handful of cilantro, chopped
  • 2 limes
  • 1/2 cup (130 g) salsa (optional)
  • 1 tsp avocado oil
  • 1 tsp each chili powder, cumin, oregano; salt

How to make it

  1. Prep everythingPat the 1 1/2 lb (680 g) chicken thighs dry with paper towels. Finely dice the 1/2 white onion into 1/4-inch (6 mm) pieces and chop the cilantro. Cut 1 lime in half for juicing and the other into wedges for serving. Count out your 12 corn tortillas.
  2. Season the chickenIn a bowl, toss the chicken with 1 tsp each chili powder, cumin and oregano, about 1 tsp salt, the 1 tsp avocado oil and the juice of 1 lime. Make sure every piece is coated.
  3. Sear the chickenHeat a large 12-inch (30 cm) cast-iron or heavy skillet over high heat for 3 minutes, until a drop of water sizzles away instantly. Lay the chicken flat and sear (cook hard and fast without moving it) 5 to 6 minutes per side, until dark brown and charred in spots. It is done at 175°F (79°C) in the thickest part.
  4. Rest and chopMove the chicken to a cutting board and rest it 5 minutes. Chop it into small 1/2-inch (1 cm) pieces and scrape any juices from the board back over the meat.
  5. Warm the tortillasWipe out the skillet and set it over medium-high heat with no oil. Warm each tortilla 30 seconds per side, until soft, bendy and lightly spotted brown. If they are thin, double them up. Stack them inside a clean kitchen towel to stay warm.
  6. Build the tacosFill each tortilla with chicken, a sprinkle of onion and cilantro, and a squeeze of lime. Spoon on some of the 1/2 cup (130 g) salsa if you like. Serve 3 tacos per person, right away.
Chef's tipCold corn tortillas crack and tear. Always warm them in a hot dry pan until they bend without breaking, and keep them wrapped in a towel until you build.
