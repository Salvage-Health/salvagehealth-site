About Salvage Health

What is Salvage Health?

Salvage Health is health and fitness education in plain English, from Bryan Dourado. The idea is simple: it's never too late to take your life back, and the way you do it is by understanding how this actually works, so you can build a plan that fits your life. It started with the book Fitness Without the Fear and is growing into articles, tools, coaching and programs.

Who is Bryan Dourado?

Bryan went from 265 to 178 pounds (120 to 81 kg) in 14 months, reversed his prediabetes, and got rid of his sleep apnea, without a medical background. He taught himself how nutrition and training actually work, then wrote the book he couldn't find when he started. He's not a doctor, and nothing here is medical advice.

Is this for me if I'm older, heavier, or dealing with health problems?

Yes. You're never too old, too sick, too heavy or too thin to do something. Start where you are, and if you have a medical condition, bring your doctor into the loop before you change how you eat or train.

The book and the app

Where can I get Fitness Without the Fear?

The audiobook is free on Spotify. The ebook is on Kindle. The paperback is coming soon to Amazon.

What is the companion app?

A free tool at salvagehealth.com/book that turns the book into your personal plan. Enter your stats and goal, and it calculates your calories, protein, fat and carbs and a realistic goal date. You can save your plan as a PDF or print the worksheet instead.

Does the app store my information?

No. Everything you enter stays on your own device. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else.

Do I need the book to use the app?

No, but they work best together. Each chapter explains the reasoning behind one section of your plan.

Coaching, supplements and medical care

Do you offer coaching?

Not yet, but it's coming. Follow Bryan on Instagram to hear first.

Do you sell supplements?

Not right now. If you're curious what's worth taking, start with Supplements: What's Worth It and What's Marketing.

Do I have to do 75 Hard?

No. It's a tool that changed Bryan's life, but it's intense and not for everyone. Read What 75 Hard Taught Me About Discipline to decide if it's right for you.

Does Salvage Health give medical advice or prescriptions?

No. Salvage Health is education only. If you want medical care with a licensed provider involved, Bryan also founded Velacore Health, a separate company that connects you with independent licensed physicians for physician-reviewed wellness programs. Any treatment is decided by the physician, not by Velacore or Salvage Health.
