Why Salvage Health exists

I needed a second chance. I didn't know how to take it.

I was 265 pounds, prediabetic, with high blood pressure and sleep apnea, and I wasn't even 40. I'd tried every diet there is. Each one worked until it didn't, and I never understood why.

What finally changed things wasn't another plan. It was understanding how this actually works: calories, protein, training, sleep, all of it. Once I understood it, I could build a plan that fit my life instead of forcing my life into someone else's plan.

Salvage Health is everything I wish someone had handed me on day one. It's for anyone who's ready for their own second chance and doesn't know where to start.
